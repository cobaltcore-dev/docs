# Greenhouse

[Greenhouse](https://cloudoperators.github.io/greenhouse/) is the monitoring and management umbrella for CobaltCore. It provides a unified operational interface that integrates observability tooling, plugin extensions, and team-level access management across the cloud infrastructure.

## Role in CobaltCore

Greenhouse aggregates operational views from multiple tools - Prometheus, Perses dashboards, and custom plugins - into a single portal for operations teams. Rather than navigating separate UIs for each component, operators access a unified interface that is structured around their organizational teams and responsibilities.

## Key capabilities

- **Plugin system** - extend Greenhouse with operational tools relevant to your team (monitoring, alerting, runbooks)
- **Team-scoped access** - permissions and views are scoped to organizational teams
- **Observability integration** - surfaces Prometheus alerts and Perses dashboards in context
- **CobaltCore integration** - Greenhouse is referenced on the CobaltCore landing page as the monitoring and management tool
